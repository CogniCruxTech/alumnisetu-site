What AlumniSetu is

A multi-tenant trust platform. Each institution verifies its own alumni; verification is what unlocks the network. It is not a social network — every feature favours verifiability and the privacy of members over engagement.

Verified identity

Alumni prove who they are through their college — a college email, a document, or a verified member's vouch.

Mentorship

Students and alumni connect for guidance, entirely within their institution.

Jobs & referrals

Verified alumni post opportunities; members apply with a real, vouched profile.

Events & community

Reunions, webinars and a college community feed for members.

Sign in

Members sign in with an email and password or with Google Sign-In. Google sign-in shares only your email address and basic profile (name) with AlumniSetu; see the Privacy Policy for exactly what we collect and why.
